I collect ideas the way some people collect tools. For years the collection lived in notebooks and folders, growing but never public. This site is the correction: a place where the thinking happens in the open, unfinished on purpose.

I write for people with real responsibility who privately suspect they're still beginners — because I'm one of them, and because I've noticed that the people building the next era of energy, compute, and institutions mostly are too. The ones worth learning from aren't the ones who stopped having questions.

So: no feeds, no urgency, no performance. Topic pages are tables where notes, essays, projects, and open questions sit together. Things get revised, and the revision dates are shown. If you read something and want to push back, [email me](mailto:harry@harrymunroe.com) — that's where the good part happens.

*Still forming, like most things here.*
